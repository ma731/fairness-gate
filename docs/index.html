What the gap means

Among people who genuinely earn above the threshold, the model finds 85.4% of the Asian alone group and 54.2% of the Some other race alone group. Accuracy is close to flat across groups, which is why accuracy is the wrong thing to look at: a model can be equally accurate everywhere and still place its errors where they matter most.
